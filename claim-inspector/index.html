Claim Inspector · Family Medicine

A reference and decision-support tool. Code sets, payer rules and Medicare policy change constantly (CPT/HCPCS yearly in January, ICD-10-CM each October, NCCI quarterly, telehealth rules frequently). Always verify against the current AMA CPT book, CMS/MAC policy and your payer contracts. Not legal or clinical advice. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere - still, do not enter patient names or other identifiers.
