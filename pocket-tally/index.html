Tap the mic and say what you spent or received

How it works

  1. Say what came in: "received 69000".
  2. Say what you keep aside: "set aside 10000". What's left is your month.
  3. Say what you spend: "coffee 40", "cab 85". Add a note any time by tapping an entry.
  4. Going on a trip, or spending someone else's money? Make an Event and it keeps its own tally.
  5. More than one phone? Settings → Connect Google Drive keeps a copy in your own Drive, in a folder only this app can see.

Everything stays on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
